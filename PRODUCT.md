# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Long-term value investors who make low-frequency position decisions inside a
defined circle of competence. They use the dashboard to review a small,
preselected universe, understand whether evidence supports buying, holding,
trimming, or exiting, and control downside risk before acting.

## Product Purpose

Turn fresh Alva market and company data into an inspectable daily decision
process. The product combines valuation discipline with independent specialist
reviews, Bull/Bear challenge, risk gates, and staged position sizing. Success
means the investor can see what changed, why an action is proposed, how strong
the evidence is, where roles disagree, and which missing evidence limits the
conclusion. It supports decisions and never places trades or promises returns.

## Brand Personality

Calm, rigorous, candid. The interface should feel like an investment committee
briefing: concise at first glance, detailed on demand, and explicit about
uncertainty.

## Anti-references

Avoid trading terminals optimized for intraday activity, dense spreadsheet-like
walls of numbers, generic AI summaries without evidence, decorative fintech
dashboards, identical scores presented as independent opinions, and interfaces
that hide dissent or overstate confidence.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the decision chain: action, decisive evidence, contrary evidence,
   and risk boundary.
2. Make disagreement visible; consensus is meaningful only when independent
   roles reached it from the same sourced evidence packet.
3. Explain every confidence value through evidence coverage, freshness,
   agreement, and explicit caps.
4. Keep the first view scannable and reveal detailed evidence progressively.
5. Separate sourced facts, deterministic calculations, Agent inference, and
   portfolio policy so the investor knows what can be trusted and challenged.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Do not rely on color alone for buy, hold, trim, risk, or
confidence states. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable
contrast, reduced-motion behavior, responsive layouts, and text alternatives
for charts and loading/error states.
