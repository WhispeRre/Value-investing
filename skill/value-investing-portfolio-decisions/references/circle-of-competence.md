# Circle Of Competence

Classify every security before research:

| Circle | Allowed output |
|---|---|
| Core | valuation, staged buy/hold/trim/exit actions, alerts |
| Learning | sourced research and knowledge gaps; no direct buy action |
| Out of Scope | context only; never a candidate or recommendation |

Passing the circle gate requires an allowed market and business model plus an
understandable revenue driver, key KPI, major risk, and suitable valuation
method. User preference alone does not establish competence.

Competitor, supplier, macro, and benchmark securities may be shown as context
without entering the candidate universe. Any new candidate must pass the gate
before other agents analyze it.
