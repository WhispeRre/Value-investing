# Valuation Policy

Use ranges rather than a single intrinsic-value point estimate. Select the
method by business model: DCF or FCFF for stable cash flows, reverse DCF for
expectation-sensitive growth, normalized multiples for cyclical companies,
P/B or dividend models for suitable financials, SOTP for multi-segment firms,
and asset or replacement value for asset-heavy firms.

Every range must show bear, base, bull assumptions, current price, margin of
safety, forecast horizon, and confidence. Revalue when material operating data
changes; do not move value merely to explain price movement.

Quality and thesis gates precede valuation action. A cheap security with weak
quality, broken economics, or inadequate evidence is `NO_ACTION` or
`THESIS_AT_RISK`, not an automatic buy.
